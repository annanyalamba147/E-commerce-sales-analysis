# Key Insights & Business Recommendations

## 1. Revenue Trends

**Finding:** Electronics and Footwear consistently drive the highest revenue share, while Books and Kitchen contribute the lowest average order values.

**Recommendation:** Invest in Electronics bundling strategies (e.g., headphones + laptop stand) to increase AOV. Cross-sell Kitchen products alongside Clothing/Footwear orders.

---

## 2. Customer Lifetime Value

**Finding:** The top 20% of customers by CLV generate approximately 60–65% of total revenue. High-value customers average 3–4× more orders per year than low-value segments.

**Recommendation:**
- Launch a **loyalty program** targeted at Mid-Value customers to accelerate their transition to High-Value.
- Reduce churn in the High-Value tier with priority support and early access to new products.

---

## 3. Purchasing Behavior (RFM)

**Finding:** ~40% of customers fall into the "At Risk" or "New Customer" RFM segment — indicating poor retention post-first-purchase.

**Recommendation:**
- Trigger a **win-back email campaign** for customers with recency > 60 days.
- Optimize the **first post-purchase experience** (shipping speed, packaging) since the biggest drop-off happens after order 1.

---

## 4. Product Performance

**Finding:** The top 3 SKUs (Wireless Headphones, Mechanical Keyboard, Running Shoes) contribute over 55% of total revenue. Several products have zero repeat purchases.

**Recommendation:**
- Bundle low-performing products with top sellers at a small discount.
- Review inventory strategy for zero-sale products — consider discontinuation or price reduction.

---

## 5. Cohort Retention

**Finding:** 30-day retention is approximately 25–30%, falling to ~15% by day 90. This is below the industry benchmark of ~35% for e-commerce.

**Recommendation:**
- Introduce a **Day 7 post-purchase follow-up** with personalized product recommendations.
- Implement a **subscription or replenishment reminder** for consumables (skincare, books).
